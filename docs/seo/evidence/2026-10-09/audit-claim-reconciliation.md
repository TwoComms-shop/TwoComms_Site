## Primary documentation used for interpreting prescriptions

- Google AI features: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features — same foundational SEO, indexed snippet-eligible pages, no special AI text files/schema required, inclusion not guaranteed.
- Google spam policy: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies — paid ranking links, keyword stuffing and low-value scaled pages can violate policy; sponsored/nofollow advertising links acceptable.
- Google hiring SEO: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo — assess promises/case context and ranking guarantees.
- Lighthouse scoring: https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/performance/performance-scoring — weighted metrics;90–100good;100extremely challenging/not expected; diminishing returns near96.
- Lazy loading: https://web.dev/articles/browser-level-image-lazy-loading — initial viewport/LCP eager; outside viewport lazy; preserve dimensions.
- CSP report-only: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Headers/Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only — monitor without enforcement; report-to/Reporting-Endpoints preferred, report-uri backward-compatible. Policy existence does not imply enforcement.
