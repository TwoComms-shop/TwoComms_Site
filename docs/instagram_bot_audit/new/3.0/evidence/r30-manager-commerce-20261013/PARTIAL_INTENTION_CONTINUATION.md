# R30 partial intention: authenticated continuation into a later ordinary Order

Design only, 2026-10-13. Read-only source audit of `/Users/zainllw0w/.codex/worktrees/r30-media-integration/site`; reconcile proposed interfaces with foundation 38 and assisted request/decision/execution implementation 39 before implementing 40. This document creates no repository model, provider effect, test result or deployment claim.

## Decision

Use the three assisted request, decision and execution-binding responsibilities already described in `CUSTOM_SCOPED_CHECKOUT_CONTRACT.md`. A stable intention UUID connects immutable full-source request versions and their actual paid execution coverage. No additional intention, continuation, wishlist, payment or shipping registry is needed.

Together waits for every requested line to become physically ready, then uses one ordinary checkout and Order. Separate checks out only the exact accepted ready whole lines now. Deferred lines remain unpaid wishes in the same full assisted request; later physical readiness plus a new authenticated customer purchase action creates a fresh commercial episode and a fresh ordinary checkout/Order linked to the same intention. Restock opt-in, manager readiness, purchase authority and payment truth stay distinct.

Important source correction: native payment does not necessarily close the first commercial episode. `ig_commercial_episodes._fulfillment_projection` (1301) returns ORDER_CREATED with no closed timestamp until actual delivery or cancellation. `bind_episode_order` (1189) occupies its one intended Order; `ig_revision_checkout.checkout_owner_scope` (98) then refuses another checkout in that owner. A later customer purchase explicitly moves the old episode's open slot and creates a clean new session; it must not mark the first Order fulfilled or paid again.

The existing historical reorder helper `ig_commerce_reorder.prepare_reorder_origin` is not the remainder producer. Its origin requires an owned physical OrderItem and (on new admission) a paid Order. A WAIT line intentionally has neither. It also rejects custom/null-product and unsupported option cases. Do not create a fake historical OrderItem to satisfy it, reuse "last purchased item" fallback, or invoke the open-line copy helper after the old cycle is no longer current.

## One complete native flow

1. Original webhook messages are reduced through native source producers, captured into the full assisted Request R1, and reviewed through authenticated manager Decision D1. R1 freezes every original line, recipient, original position, quantity/default authority, product/custom configuration evidence, gift/deadline facts, source revision/session/episode/reset namespace and their digests. Stable intention UUID I is published through the existing assisted current head.
2. D1 has A READY and B WAIT. The existing/native approved split offer displays both exact lines, timing uncertainty, per-scope delivery payer and accepted maximum or exact fee, goods due now, and B as unpaid/deferred. Only its actual delivered receipt plus authenticated customer separate choice permits Execution E1 selecting A and deferring B. If separate choice text does not itself explicitly authorize purchase, require separate purchase evidence; never infer it from logistics consent alone.
3. E1 owns the ordinary proposal and one winner series. On provider-verified settlement, it binds actual Order O1 and exact OrderItem map. Under the intention/client barrier its settled coverage records A; B stays in R1. Existing payment owners remain responsible for settlement and side effects; group coverage is a projection of sealed execution/order binding, not a payment boolean or a copied manager statement.
4. Once B may be available, stock accounting or notification alone does not authorize checkout. Record fresh manager Decision D2 for the same current full intention version. D2 names B's exact original line/recipient/configuration/quantity and independent price approval; supply basis must be physical ready supply, not incoming_confirmed, with freshness no longer than existing 24 hours. Keep A's settled disposition explicit but do not reapprove it for execution. Finite catalog policy/protected stock and any necessary explicit absolute recount are separate admission. Custom readiness uses the finite authenticated assisted approval from 39.
5. If B's accepted notification subscription is active, send the existing restock notification only through its permission/window/receipt owners and fresh manager gate. No new episode, cart or invoice is created by that send. Without explicit opt-in do not schedule/send a restock message merely because separate was chosen. If customer independently returns, process their authenticated request normally.
6. Display/send a bounded continuation card for B with actual D2-approved current price, exact configuration/qty/recipient, preserved original intention I, reference to O1 without implying B was bought, timing/deadline uncertainty, and B's current delivery payer/cost. The card explicitly offers a new order for these remaining lines. Existing revision/outbox effects own native delivery evidence; its descriptor is frozen on assisted decision/execution evidence, not a fourth offer registry. Bind card body/buttons digest, native MID, owning effect/task/revision, SENT completeness, decision/request/version and expiry. UNKNOWN is not an acceptable receipt and never blindly resends.
7. Customer presses the signed native continuation purchase button after that exact delivered card. `owned_source` authenticates current new source S2 and sealed revision. The continuation validator rejects mixed corrections/attachments/arbitrary same-text button tokens, foreign sender, stale version, unmatched reply/receipt, expired manager readiness/card, settled or still-inflight selected lines, changed recipient/deadline/configuration, reset/erasure/namespace/permission change and ambiguous current purchase ownership.
8. Under the existing commercial client barrier and DB transaction, create/reuse the new repeat episode using S2 as fresh evidence and open its empty session. Append one native continuation transition adopting only B as a new wish; it carries original history proof and S2 adoption proof separately. It does not carry D1/D2 prices into ordinary source customer-choice fields, nor contacts, shipment, receipt, paid state or allocation. Keep recipient explicit. For whole-line scope retain original intention line identity as the group identity and map it to a new-session line identity (e.g. `line:<S2.pk>:<ordinal>`), with original and local positions separate. Do not retarget old line identity to another recipient.
9. Capture the new session through a continuation-aware native projection. Seal Request R2 as the next version under I with a parent R1 descriptor, full original intention origin and conserved coverage, plus current adopted residual capture/owner and S2 proof. If unchanged remainder terms were already physically reviewed by D2, transfer only through a verified owner-transition receipt binding that exact target, approved price/quantity, timestamp and decision digest; otherwise obtain fresh D3 after R2. Either representation must leave original D2 immutable and export one unambiguous manager authority for E2. Do not automatically extend freshness during transition.
10. Prepare E2 on the new owner selecting B, with prior-settled A, no silent omissions and fresh quote/delivery/purchase approval. Existing normal proposal, hold, invoice and winner create O2. E2 binds O2 to I and the original B identity. Client and admin project O1/O2 payment, items and shipment independently, and I's remaining/settled coverage from the same requests/executions.

The minimal first implementation may require a fresh manager approval after R2 and avoid transfer of D2 readiness across owners. This is slower but concrete and safe. A transfer helper is optional; if absent, block checkout pending that review instead of treating historical D2 as current on a rewritten request.

## Historical origin, current owner and coverage proof

Use bounded sealed payloads on the three existing assisted responsibilities. Suggested fields are contracts to align with foundation, not an alternate schema:

```
request identity: intention_uuid, source_version, request_id, request_digest
parent: parent_request_id/version/digest, original_full_capture_digest
origin scope: client_id, original_episode_id, session_id, generation,
              selection_revision, source_revision_id/digest, reset_id/floor,
              namespace, permission_generation, original source IDs/digests
origin lines: original_line_id, original_index, recipient_id,
              exact configuration/qty/default authority, field origin proofs
requirements: source-bound recipient/gift/deadline values + digest/version
current owner: episode_id, session_id, generation, selection_revision,
               current source_capture_digest and owner fence
adoption: new_source MID/id/digest/provider timestamp, frozen revision proof,
          action payload digest, actual card/receipt/effect digest, accepted_at,
          old -> new scope mapping, explicit selected identities
manager: exact decision id/version/digest, actor, physically_checked_at,
         checked quantity/supply basis, configuration and price digest, expires
coverage: prior-settled execution/order/provenance descriptors,
          selected-now identities, deferred identities, explicitly-cancelled identities
delivery: parent split-choice/receipt digest, parcel/scope identity,
          payer, quoted amount or customer cap, currency, included/prepaid mode,
          current terms digest, fresh acceptance when changed
execution: proposal/revision/series identity, execution digest and terminal state,
           canonical winner/Order and exact selected OrderItem map on settlement
```

At every new execution, original requested lines = proven settled + selected now + deferred + explicitly cancelled, with unique disjoint identities and exact conserved quantities. Refund is a payment disposition; it does not silently make an originally fulfilled line eligible for another charge. If customer wants a new replacement/repeat of a settled line, create an explicit new intention rather than removing settled coverage. Partial quantity slices are out of this minimal whole-line scope: require separately reviewed source sublines or abstain.

Coverage is recalculated from authoritative terminal execution/order provenance and in-flight executions under the stable intention lock; copied JSON coverage is only a snapshot to compare. A later paid callback for an older competing scope must be visible and block a new overlap until reconciliation. A new decision version must not restore finite capacity already consumed by E1. Decision/proposal OneToOne plus intention active-slot guard alone is insufficient unless overlap with all historical settled and unresolved paid executions is checked.

The old full capture remains on immutable R1 after current selection rollover. Do not require `capture_current_selection_lines` to recover it; that API intentionally reads only open current state. Historical origin verification checks the stored request seal and its genuine source/transition proofs (without asserting current episode equality), existing original source edits/erasure/reset permission rules, and ancestry current-head/CAS. New S2 remains strictly current authenticated authority. A request digest proves immutable bytes, not continuing authorization by itself.

When new correction explicitly targets a deferred original line, append a new full request version and invalidate pending old card/manager/execution proofs. A change in recipient must invalidate any inherited size/fit preferences as the native reducer already does. Deadline changes/withdrawal are source-bound revisions independent of catalog configuration digest. Old deadline stays visible as historical; a passed date never becomes a promised new date. A new unrelated purchase must not silently erase the separate deferred intention or become its continuation. Multiple candidate residual intentions require exact native card/reference or clarification.

## Transaction boundaries and crash recovery

Continuation native admission authenticates settings/client/revision/source and receipt first; under the canonical client/commercial barrier acquire intent/request/current-decision/execution locks in foundation's agreed order, recompute paid/in-flight coverage and CAS, and then create/reuse episode, session transition and new request/owner-transition receipt atomically. New source id/action digest is the dedupe key; conflicting replay fails and cannot start another episode. If an unrelated active editable purchase exists, abstain rather than clobber it with `start_repeat_episode`.

The signed continuation purchase is source-owned; a crash after episode adoption but before proposal preparation must resume from the exact native receipt/Request R2, not start another episode. If later quote preparation fails due to stock, D2 expiry or price, preserve R2 wish and require refreshed terms instead of rolling historical coverage back. Prefer one atomic preparation after all independent price/resource validation when permitted by existing lock order. All external sends/invoice calls stay outside DB transactions under existing outbox/generation leases.

At provider settlement, preserve paid truth even if the intention has since changed. Let existing generation winner/reconciliation rules materialize or hold payment review; attach a winning actual Order and selected settled coverage in the same canonical transaction where possible. If attachment uses an on-commit recovery seam, derive coverage from verified attempt/order winner in the meantime so lost wakeups cannot free paid capacity. No queue success or callback HTTP 200 proves an Order binding.

## No-shortcut gates

If continuation native projection is not implemented, require the customer to restate exact residual item/recipient/configuration in a fresh ordinary source capture and link it to the old intention only through reviewed original-line matching; do not silently copy old wishlist fields. If assisted v2 owner ancestry is not implemented, block later assisted checkout rather than rewriting old episode IDs. If source/deferred coverage is not provable, no new charge. If physical READY or current price/cost consent is missing, keep a visible waiting/review state. None of these limitations justify inventing stock, charging WAIT goods or editing the first provider-paid Order.
