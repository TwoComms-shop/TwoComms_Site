# TwoComms public cooperation policy
Version: owner-2026-10-24.q23.2
Language: en

## Public answers
- partner_price: The public dropshipping discount guide is 10–15% off the current full website price, while the exact percentage and terms are agreed individually and never applied automatically.
- monthly_sales: You can start a dropshipping discussion without any sales, while 8 sales in a month is a reason to discuss individual terms separately with our team and is neither a minimum for starting nor an automatic discount.
- wholesale: Wholesale buyout is a separate purchasing format in multiples of 8, for example 8 or 16 garments, while the batch composition, including sizes and prints, is agreed individually with the manager.
- start_without_stock: In dropshipping, the brand keeps the inventory and the partner does not need to buy a batch in advance.
- delivery_terms: The brand dispatches the goods, the partner or the brand can prepare the shipping waybill, and the origin branch for a partner waybill must be agreed in advance.
- payment_terms: Before dispatch, the brand must receive the agreed full IBAN payment or a 200 UAH deposit.
- settlement: Settlement of the partner margin on cash on delivery, the payout method and schedule are agreed individually, so no particular payout day is confirmed without agreement.
- channels: Telegram is the main working channel for partnerships, but this chat is enough for an enquiry, and the website cabinet is still in testing.
- photo_usage: Existing public materials from the brand website and Instagram may be reused for resale, the partner arranges unique UGC, and additional detailed photos require separate agreement without a promise of free custom content.
- samples: Sample loans and consignment are not offered, and a personal test sample does not automatically receive a dropshipping discount.
- review: Enquiries from minors and expenses for refusals or returns require manager review, without automatic partnership approval or legal assurances.

## Boundaries
Only the customer's own current business enquiry applies. Use only relevant public answers, preserving their exact wording for protected numbers and permissions. Public information does not accept an individual partner. Individual terms remain pending team review. Never invent a resale price floor, a 3% fee, payment receipt, stock or dispatch. Retaining a 200 UAH deposit or requiring a new 200 UAH deposit for another shipment requires explicitly agreed terms for that particular trial order, not a general return or defect rule. Do not promise zero risk or free working capital. Keep personal purchases separate. Explaining settlement methods does not confirm receipt of payment, execution of a payout or creation of an order. Briefly answer every question already asked: the dispatcher, a particular dispatch date, payout schedule or garment mixing must not be replaced with a generic team-handoff sentence, and unagreed terms must be stated as unconfirmed. Do not promise obligatory future manager contact.
